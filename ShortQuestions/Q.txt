Q: Compare different types of advices in Spring AOP.

Advice:
@Before:	          Before method     	      Validation
@After: 	          After method (always)   	Cleanup
@AfterReturning:  	On success	              Logging result
@AfterThrowing:   	On exception	            Error logging
@Around:        	  Before & After	          Performance monitoring
